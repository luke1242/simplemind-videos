# Current video: "The Biggest Regret Almost Everyone Has"

**Topic:** regrets of inaction. Over time, people regret what they *didn't* do far more than what they did.
**Series:** this is episode 2 after the spotlight video, and it calls back to it twice.
**Length:** the raw voice is about 1,470 words. After pause padding, the target is about **10:00**.
**Audio:** `audio/part1.mp3` … `audio/part4.mp3` (ElevenLabs, from Luke).
**Music:** `music/track.mp3`, mixed softly as before.

## How to read this list

- **picture:** the main picture for the scene.
  - `NEW` means Luke makes it (prompt in `prompts/nano-banana-prompts.md`).
  - `COPY` means it's already in `pictures/` (reused from video 1 and renamed).
  - `SAME AS Sxx` means reuse that scene's picture again in this video.
  - `CODED` means you build it in code (see "New diagram kinds" at the bottom).
- **beats:** the key moments, with the exact trigger word in quotes. Fill the remaining time with crops, cards, icons and pop-ups as the CLAUDE.md pacing rules say (every shot ≤ 4s).
  - `icon Ixx "word"` is an icon cutaway.
  - `pop Nxx "word" → content` is a narrator pop-up.
  - `card "word" → TEXT` is a text card. **Bold** words go in a yellow badge and ~~struck~~ words in a red badge.
  - `step "word" → STEP n + TEXT` is the top-center step pill.
  - `crop "word" → subject` is a punch-in crop of the main picture.
- **narration** is used for timing only and must match the audio. Never show it on screen.
- **Icons:** I06–I30 are reused from video 1. I40–I50 are new (Luke makes them; prompts in `prompts/icon-prompts.md`). If an icon file is missing, skip that beat.

## New diagram kinds to add to `tools/build-part.mjs`

Use the same look as the existing pie and scale: cream background, 9px ink outlines, Nunito ExtraBold, and yellow or red outlined badges. Every animation must be seek-safe on the GSAP timeline.

- **grid:** 60 simple person icons (round head plus rounded body, ink outline, white fill) in 6 rows × 10. Options: `fill` (how many turn red), `word` (when they turn, row by row over 0.6s), `label` (badge text under the grid). Used in S14 and S16.
- **bars:** two vertical bars side by side, labels under them, and percentages in badges on top. They grow from 0 over 0.6s (`power2.out`) on `word`. The first bar is red and the second is Shadow grey `#E6E1D6`. Used in S19 and S41.
- **lines:** a chart titled "REGRET OVER TIME" with an x-axis from TODAY to YEARS LATER and no y numbers. Line A, "THINGS I DID" (terracotta), starts high and falls. Line B, "THINGS I DIDN'T DO" (red), starts low, rises and crosses A at about 40%. Draw both lines with a stroke-dashoffset tween over 1.5s on `word`. FADE and GROW tags pop next to the line ends on their words. Used in S24 and S45.
- **split:** two halves with a thin vertical ink divider. Each side holds a short line of badge text and appears on its own word. Used in S46.
- **quote:** a large opening quote mark in soft orange, then the quote in sentence case at about 64px, centered and max 1500px wide, with "courage" and "true to myself" in yellow badges. It may stay on screen up to **7s** (the one exception to the 4s rule). Small text under it: "Bronnie Ware, palliative care nurse". Used in S42.
- **iconRow:** three icons from `icons/` in a row on cream, each popping in (0.2s, 0.92 → 1.0) on its own word. It counts as one shot. Used in S48.
- **card → card swap:** like `diagramB` for the pie, a hard cut to card B on the trigger word. Used in S53.
- **Icon as main picture (S31, S32):** prepare it like a picture (scale the 16:9 icon to 1920×1080 with lanczos).
