# Icon cutaway prompts (fast-cut "spot illustrations")

These are the quick in-between shots, like the stressed guy with the checklist or the building with a pin. They let the video change pictures every 3 to 4 seconds.

## How to use

1. Open a **new** Nano Banana chat. Attach your 2 example icon images (the checklist guy and the building with the pin) plus `reference/characters/narrator.png`.
2. Paste the **SETUP** message.
3. Paste the prompts one at a time, choosing **2K** for each. Save them as `I01.jpg`, `I02.jpg`, and so on, with the exact names below.
4. Upload them to a folder called `icons/` on GitHub (or send them to Claude in chat).

## SETUP

```
I'm making quick "cutaway" icons for a YouTube explainer. Every image in this chat must match the attached example icons exactly:
one simple centered subject on a plain off-white background (#FAF7F0), clean thick black outlines, flat colors with a little soft shading, lots of empty space around the subject, friendly and simple. 16:9, 2K. NO text, NO words, NO letters, NO numbers anywhere.
Stick figures are simple stickmen with round grey or white heads and black stick limbs, like the example.
When I say "the narrator", use the attached narrator exactly: a glowing peach brain head with two white oval eyes and no mouth, a black hoodie and pants, and white sneakers with a red stripe.
Reply "ready" and wait for my first prompt.
```
