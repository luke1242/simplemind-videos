# Nano Banana picture prompts: "The Biggest Regret Almost Everyone Has"

**24 new pictures.** The other 13 are reused from the spotlight video and are already in place.

## How to use

1. Open **one** new Nano Banana chat, so everything stays consistent.
2. **Attach:** `reference/characters/narrator.png` plus 2 or 3 pictures from your spotlight video as style examples (for example S08 bedroom, S12 T-shirt student, S27 cafeteria).
3. Paste the **SETUP** message and wait for "ready".
4. Paste the prompts **one at a time**, in this order. Choose **2K** for each one.
5. Download each image and name it **exactly** as shown (e.g. `S01.png`).
6. Prompts marked **(edit of …)** are "same image, but…" edits. Paste them right after the picture they edit.
7. If the narrator drifts, reply: *"Redo it. The narrator must match the reference exactly: glowing peach brain head, two white oval eyes, no mouth, black hoodie, black pants, white sneakers with a red stripe."*
8. Send them all to Claude in a chat (a zip works), and Claude adds them to GitHub.

## SETUP (paste first, with the reference images attached)

```
I'm making a YouTube explainer video. Every image I ask for in this chat must use the SAME style and the SAME main character as the attached images. Please lock these in:

STYLE (match the attached pictures): 2D comic-style illustration, clean thick black outlines on everything, warm earthy colors (ochre, sandy yellow, terracotta, olive green, warm sky blue, sunset orange), light hatching and soft shading on objects and backgrounds, fully illustrated environments with a sense of place, friendly and simple. 16:9 widescreen, 2K. NO text, NO words, NO letters, NO numbers anywhere in the images.

MAIN CHARACTER "the narrator" (match the attached narrator image exactly): a tall, slim figure whose head is a large smooth cartoon BRAIN in warm peach-pink with soft fold lines and a soft peach glow, two small glowing white oval eyes, NO mouth, a short black neck, a black hoodie with drawstrings (hood down), slim black pants, black hands, and white sneakers with a thin red stripe. He never changes design. Emotion is shown only through his eye shapes and his body pose.

SIDE CHARACTERS: classic stickmen like the attached pictures: round white heads, dot eyes, expressive curved eyebrows, simple mouths, black stick limbs, simple flat-colored clothes (sky blue, sage green or terracotta shirts).

Reply "ready" and wait for my first prompt.
```
