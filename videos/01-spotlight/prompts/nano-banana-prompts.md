# Nano Banana image prompts: "One of the Most Useful Lessons I Learned"

## How to use this

1. Open **one** new Nano Banana chat. Keep every image in this same chat so the character stays consistent.
2. **Attach:** `reference/characters/narrator.png` plus 2 or 3 of the `reference/style/target-*.png` screenshots.
3. Paste the **SETUP** message. Wait for it to confirm or make the test image.
4. Then paste the prompts **one at a time**. Download each image and name it exactly as shown (e.g. `S01.png`).
5. If the narrator drifts (wrong head, wrong colors), reply: *"Redo it. The narrator must match the reference exactly: glowing peach brain head, two white oval eyes, no mouth, black hoodie, black pants, white sneakers with a red stripe."*
6. Upload all the images to GitHub in a folder called `pictures/`.

**Prompts marked with "B"** are edits of the image right before them. Nano Banana is great at "same image, but…" edits.

## SETUP (paste first, with the reference images attached)

```
I'm making a YouTube explainer video. Every image I ask for in this chat must use the SAME style and the SAME main character. Please lock these in:

STYLE (match the attached screenshots): 2D comic-style illustration, clean thick black outlines on everything, warm earthy colors (ochre, sandy yellow, terracotta, olive green, warm sky blue, sunset orange), light hatching and soft shading on objects and backgrounds, fully illustrated environments with a sense of place, friendly and simple. 16:9 widescreen, 1920x1080. NO text, NO words, NO letters anywhere in the images.

MAIN CHARACTER "the narrator" (match the attached narrator image exactly): a tall, slim figure whose head is a large smooth cartoon BRAIN in warm peach-pink with soft fold lines and a soft peach glow around it, two small glowing white oval eyes, NO mouth, a short black neck, a black hoodie with drawstrings (hood down), slim black pants, black hands, and white sneakers with a thin red stripe. He never changes design. Emotion is shown only through his eye shapes and his body pose.

SIDE CHARACTERS: classic stickmen like the screenshots: round white heads, dot eyes, expressive curved eyebrows, simple mouths, black stick limbs, simple flat-colored clothes (sky blue, sage green or terracotta shirts), modern everyday clothes.

To confirm, make a test image: the narrator standing in a simple sunny park, front view.
```
